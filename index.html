The short version

Started in 1954.
Never really changed the kitchen.

Oberoi Dhaba Adchini has been feeding this part of Delhi since 1954. The tandoor still runs on the same fire it always has. Everything else grew up around it, a full bar, a rooftop, sushi and wood-fired pizza sitting on the same menu as the dal makhni. Same dhaba. Much bigger night.
